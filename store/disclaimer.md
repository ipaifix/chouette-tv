# Content disclaimer

**Short (in-app, About/Settings):**
> Chouette TV is a player only. It ships with no channels, playlists, streams or accounts. You configure your own IPTV source and are responsible for the rights to the content you access.

**Long (store listing / reviewer notes):**
> Chouette TV is an IPTV player for Samsung Smart TV. It contains no bundled channels, default playlist or pre-configured source accounts. On first launch, users configure their own source in Settings. The project does not host or supply streams. Content rights and access conditions depend on the user's sources.

Reviewer testing must use a separately verified, authorized test source. The
historical reviewer playlist is excluded from the public export; availability
and redistribution rights of its old URLs have not been validated for this release.
